

## v1 reasoning and survival prompts (visible development material, not a held-out benchmark)

1. How does a state differ from an observation and provider acknowledgment?
2. Why is posterior probability conditional on the supplied transition and observation model?
3. What does a zero-evidence observation require the researcher to do?
4. Write the finite-horizon Bellman equation and state its required Markov assumption.
5. Can a mathematical policy optimize a reward that is unrelated to the true task? Give a specific counterexample.
6. Show a graph where inadmissible A* heuristic returns a costlier path than Dijkstra.
7. Why does a tool timeout after sending a mutation not authorize a new transaction ID?
8. What conditions distinguish temporary worker failure from operator cancellation?
9. When can two contradictory memory entries both be valid within different contexts?
10. Why is successful worker output different from admitted artifact and verified final outcome?
11. Which paired-task observations are lost by counting only successful runs?
12. When does the inverse-propensity estimator become undefined or high variance?
13. Why is effective sample size not a proof of causality?
14. Which trace fields can be preserved while minimizing private/untrusted content?
15. Why is a SHA-256 digest not an authentic digital signature?
16. Which three steps let a human reconstruct the belief update offline from this package?
17. What does a source URL fail to preserve if the internet disappears?
18. Which public source rights restrict mirroring papers into a knowledge vault?
19. What is the difference between content-ready v1 and independent scientific approval?
20. When does Agent Atlas hand off to Computation and when is the router developed?
