# 67. Scoped scientific content v1: reconstruction versus certification

**Theme:** Research governance · **Chemistry Atlas scoped research v1.0.0**

**Evidence level:** project-written mathematical/reference explanation with scope-limited official sources. Every worked numeric input is hypothetical; publisher originals are not in this archive.

## Scientific foundation

A scoped content milestone establishes that the Atlas is a coherent, navigable, offline teaching reference with explicit equations, source-linked claims, runnable examples, failure cases and preservation procedures. It is not the final scientific validation of those explanations. Some parts of chemistry are well established but the coverage of all known processes is enormous; a single edition cannot substitute for research literature or accredited laboratories. The appropriate survival objective is the minimum sufficient kernel that lets future humans reconstruct foundational principles, calibrate measurements, rebuild tools and resume improving AI and technology.

## Governing relationship and dimensional conventions

`Evidence ladder: authored original explanation → source-scoped theory → independently coded mathematical verification → authenticated original publisher bytes → repeatable laboratory observations → qualified independent review → operational certification.`

Dimensionless activities and properly normalized state conventions are required before taking logarithms. All heat-capacity and carrier-density inputs must carry the declared temperature, phase and units.

## Reconstruction and derivation

The ladder separates distinct kinds of evidence. A unit test proves the program matches specified examples, not that a physical material follows the model under real conditions. A source-card DOI is a locator, not proof that an original document has been archived. A matching SHA-256 confirms byte identity relative to a retained reference, but a caller-supplied hash cannot authenticate scientific authority. Validated production claims require independent sample data, calibration, safety procedures and appropriate experts. The v1 release records these missing gates in the machine-readable checkpoint and science-coverage matrix.

## Original worked investigation

A hypothetical reader opens the offline manual, derives mole/charge conservation, calculates a Shomate integral and checks an ideal diode's zero-bias result. These tasks demonstrate textbook reconstructibility. To establish technological reality, the reader must next find rights-cleared original data, perform independent material tests, quantify uncertainty and compare actual apparatus to theory. None of those field steps is claimed completed here.

**Evidence requirement:** reproduce this arithmetic by hand or with independently written code; do not assert a laboratory result from an authored fixture.

## Failure boundaries and uncertainty

Authenticated primary publisher bytes and human peer review remain missing. No claims of demonstrated new materials, medical safety, industrial synthesis or completed computing reconstruction can be inferred from this edition.

An incomplete dataset, untested thermodynamic fit or missing device safety requirement must remain explicitly unknown. No hazardous synthesis, energized apparatus or chemical processing steps are specified by this reference.
