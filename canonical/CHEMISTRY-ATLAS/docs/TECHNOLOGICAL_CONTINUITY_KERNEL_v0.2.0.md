# Chemistry Atlas v0.2 — minimum sufficient technological knowledge kernel

This is a source-qualified scientific reference **for continuing to create advanced computing and AI**, not a survivalist handbook. Its mission is to preserve the reasoning, equations, constraints, tests, and experiment records needed to regenerate technology from a limited scientific base.

1. SI units, periodic structure, chemical identity and compound representations.
2. Stoichiometry with exact elemental and formal-charge conservation, and explicit nonuniqueness refusals.
3. Chemical potential, standard states, Gibbs free energy, equilibrium, kinetics and activity effects.
4. Electrochemical potential, electron bookkeeping and real battery limits (no unsafe charge procedure).
5. Semiconductor carrier neutrality, crystal/oxide interfaces, transport and idealized diffusion.
6. Spectroscopic measurement assumptions, material purity, process mass accountability and uncertainty.
7. Structured molecular/material dataset provenance and test-set leakage controls for scientific AI.
8. Rights-cleared publisher source acquisition, trustworthy hashes and offline restoration tests.

The chain does not supply an actual transistor design, precision lithography system, chip factory, compiler, or trained neural model. Those require dedicated Electronics, Manufacturing, Computer Architecture, Compiler and Neural Architecture Atlases—high priorities immediately after Chemistry and Mathematics/Physics.

**Current gap:** no original publisher primary source bytes authenticated; no expert-reviewed chemical dataset or actual semiconductor experiment. Independent numerical consistency is not a breakthrough claim.
