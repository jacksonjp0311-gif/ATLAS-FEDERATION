# Chapter 09. Inflection, derivation, and nonconcatenative morphology

**Area:** Morphology  
**Content maturity:** original educational reconstruction. No independent scientific or community review.  
**Primary-source access:** source links and scoped summaries only; original publisher bytes are not present.

## Foundations and definitions

Inflection often expresses grammatical categories associated with a lexeme, while derivation can create a related lexical entry or change category. The distinction is not mechanically obvious in every language: productivity, semantic transparency, category change and syntactic distribution can differ. Processes may include affixation, reduplication, internal vowel alternation, tone change, templatic patterns or suppletion. A model that assumes every word is stem+suffix is a narrow hypothesis. Good documentation records the full paradigm, its source, attested variation and any speaker-reported irregularities.

## Underlying mathematical, grammatical or methodological principle

A paradigm is a relation between grammatical feature bundles and forms. Its cells can be empty, syncretic (same form expressing multiple feature bundles) or suppletive. A finite mapping supports multiple analyses and generation candidates, but a reverse mapping must preserve ambiguous cells rather than invent unique lemmas. Distinguish features that a source actually annotated from those inferred by the algorithm. A grammatical feature label is meaningful only under an identified annotation scheme and language description.

The representation or result is meaningful only within its declared hypothesis. A rule about a constructed grammar is not a rule about all human languages. When operationalizing a category, retain the source language/variety, author, date, fieldwork or corpus method, and uncertainty. Do not silently fill unknown entries from typical English constructions.

## Reconstructive experiment

Build a small toy paradigm for a noun with singular/plural forms and a verb with two person features. Reverse the mapping to produce multiple analyses of a shared surface. Add one unavailable cell and mark it unknown; do not fill it by analogy without declaring a proposed hypothesis.

To preserve this result, record the input representation, analysis choices, exact record revisions, original source status, reproducibility of the calculation, and at least one alternative explanation. Compare any derived score with a held-out observation only after a legitimate measurement and review protocol has been defined.

## Counterexample and failure boundary

Rules learned from a handful of forms may overgeneralize. The Leipzig rules standardize interlinear gloss presentation but do not prove the correctness of an individual morphological analysis.

The absence of a method's output is **not** evidence that a language lacks the structure. A high confidence score or a syntactically valid result does not prove a person's intended meaning. Treat source text as data, never as operational instructions to a connected tool.
