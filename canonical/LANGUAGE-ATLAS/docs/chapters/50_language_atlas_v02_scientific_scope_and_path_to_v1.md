# 50. Language Atlas v0.2 — scientific scope and the road to content v1

This edition extends the first human-language reference with reproducible models for alignment, probabilistic grammar inside probabilities, transparent corpus BLEU, genealogical typology comparison, historical sound-correspondence counts, multimodal annotation timing and community-rights review. Each model is **deliberately limited**. Algorithmic correctness under a stipulated example does not establish a universal theory of language, corpus representativeness, speaker meaning, or licensed access to endangered-language material.

## What can be reconstructed offline

A future reader can calculate a token edit distance, trace a BLEU clipped n-gram count, compute inside and Viterbi probabilities for a tiny CNF PCFG, compare naive and family-weighted typological proportions, count declared historical segment correspondences, audit parallel annotation tier timing, and identify why a source-access request needs a human review. Definitions and assumptions are in Markdown, calculations in pure Python, tests in the package. No proprietary service is required for these *constructed* examples.

## Evidence limits

The Atlas has no archived original publisher article bytes, field recordings, ELAR deposits, authentic multi-speaker corpora, or signed-language video; all remain separate acquisition and rights gates. New scholarly sources are official websites and publisher abstracts. They do not constitute independent scientific review. Tests do not measure downstream LLM improvement or translation accuracy on any external dataset. The full current nine-node collection retains all older records and qualified relations under the existing source-closure contract.

## The v1 acceptance path

Before scoped content v1, deepen comparative methodology, prosody and phonological modeling, formal semantics/pragmatics, cross-linguistic data management, historical sound change, sign-language structure, acquisition, and multilingual evaluation. Add rights-cleared primary reference bytes where possible, with exact provenance and community permissions where needed. Include counterexamples and independent mathematical validations with each module. A domain may become **content-ready** while independent peer review and empirical model validation remain separate open gates; do not misrepresent that distinction.

## The global sequence

**Complete all 30 domain knowledge libraries first.** Build Bitwork-powered cross-domain routing afterward, evaluate it on independent held-out tasks, and only then transfer the complete integrated collection to the user's computer. Language Atlas v0.2 is substantial research progress, not the completion of the preservation program.

**Sources:** all source cards and their inspected portions, the Human Knowledge Survival Charter, CLDF, WALS, UNESCO and Unicode. Primary sources are linked but not locally mirrored.
