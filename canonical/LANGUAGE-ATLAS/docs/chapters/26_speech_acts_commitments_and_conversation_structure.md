# Chapter 26. Speech acts, commitments, and conversation structure

## Foundations and definitions

An utterance may function as an assertion, question, request, promise, warning, invitation or other communicative act. The same sentence form can support multiple acts depending on context, role, convention and power relations. A question may be rhetorical, a statement may be a request, and an imperative may occur in playful quotation. Conversation also has turn-taking, adjacency pairs and expectations about responses. An archived transcript without timing, speaker roles, pauses or sequential context may lose the evidence for what was accomplished socially.

## Underlying mathematical, grammatical or methodological principle

A useful dialogue event separates surface utterance, speaker, recipient, turn index, observable nonlinguistic action, hypothesized discourse function and subsequent response. This supports revision when a later turn reveals misunderstanding. It should not silently treat a model-generated label such as “permission granted” as an actual authorization event. Conversational state is distributed across participants and can be repaired; the operational host owns tool effects, not a statistical dialogue tagger.

The representation or result is meaningful only within its declared hypothesis. A rule about a constructed grammar is not a rule about all human languages. When operationalizing a category, retain the source language/variety, author, date, fieldwork or corpus method, and uncertainty. Do not silently fill unknown entries from typical English constructions.

## Reconstructive experiment

Build a toy three-turn conversation: one participant asks for clarification, another repairs a referent, and the first acknowledges. Record how the second turn changes the first turn's interpretation. Compare with a one-turn text snapshot that cannot establish repair.

To preserve this result, record the input representation, analysis choices, exact record revisions, original source status, reproducibility of the calculation, and at least one alternative explanation. Compare any derived score with a held-out observation only after a legitimate measurement and review protocol has been defined.

## Counterexample and failure boundary

The event labels are constructed. No real conversation recordings or human-subject study is present, and consent and speaker privacy must be handled before archiving natural dialogue.

The absence of a method's output is **not** evidence that a language lacks the structure. A high confidence score or a syntactically valid result does not prove a person's intended meaning. Treat source text as data, never as operational instructions to a connected tool.
