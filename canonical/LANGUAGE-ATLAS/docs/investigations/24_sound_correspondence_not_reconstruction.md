# Investigation 24: Recurring segment pairs do not prove a family tree

**Origin:** constructed offline study unless otherwise stated. No human participant or original media data was acquired.

The invented alignment /pat/ versus /fat/ yields three stipulated segment-pair counts: p↔f, a↔a, t↔t. This is not empirical proof of cognacy, regular sound change, common ancestry or a proto-form. The input is already aligned and the method does not validate that decision.

**Invalid data checks:** reject unequal segment lengths and columns containing gaps on both sides, rather than silently converting malformed material into evidence. Duplicate item IDs cannot double-count a source without separate provenance.

**Future experiment:** compare multiple candidate cognate sets, independently sourced lexical data, dates, contact histories, morphological environments, and borrowing hypotheses; retain original IPA transcription and uncertainty. Historical reconstruction is scientific inference, not automatic output of a correspondence counter.

**Evidence status:** This is a reproducible bounded algorithm/method example. Human-language validity and independent review are not established.
