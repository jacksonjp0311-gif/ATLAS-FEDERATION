# Investigation 26: Parallel signed-language channels are not transcription errors

**Origin:** constructed offline study unless otherwise stated. No human participant or original media data was acquired.

Two constructed annotation intervals overlap: a hands tier from 0 to 250 ms and a facial-expression tier from 100 to 200 ms. Their simultaneous timing is permitted by the data model. The audit reports **within-tier** overlap but does not forbid across-tier concurrency. Even within-tier overlaps may be purposeful alternatives or complex analyses.

**What is not inferred:** the actual handshape, facial grammar, orientation, signing space, intended meaning or signer identity. No authentic video is present. A sequence of English gloss words is insufficient to reconstruct visual language structure.

**Field protocol:** preserve original source media only under valid rights; record frame rate and synchronization, tier definitions, time basis, community-approved vocabulary, analysis disagreements and accessible playback. The toy code establishes timing arithmetic only.

**Evidence status:** This is a reproducible bounded algorithm/method example. Human-language validity and independent review are not established.
