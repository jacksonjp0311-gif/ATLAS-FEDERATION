# Language Atlas Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 14 November 2026

Initial substantive scientific reference edition: 38 original linguistic chapters, 19 constructed executable investigations, 25 source cards, 167 versioned records, 162 standalone evidence relationships and 5 additional cross-domain edges in the complete federation. Topics span speech, sign, phonology, morphology, syntax, semantics, discourse, typology, acquisition, sociolinguistics, historical change, documentation, Unicode, BCP47, corpora and language model evaluation.

The methods perform bounded toy/metadata analyses. They do not implement complete Unicode segmentation, natural-language parsing, multilingual translation, speech recognition, sign recognition, a trained language model or live Bitwork routing. No original primary-source corpus/recordings or independent linguistic review completed.


## 0.2.0 — 14 November 2026

- Added 12 new chapters and 11 author-created research investigations for a total of 50 chapters and 30 investigations.
- Added 8 publisher source cards and 57 sealed records, growing Language to 224 records and 222 local/227 full relationships.
- Added seven offline bounded method models: token alignment, corpus BLEU, PCFG inside/Viterbi, typology by family, segment correspondences, community metadata review and multimodal tiers.
- New Unicode 18 revision-49, WALS sampling, CLDF provenance, translation-scoring and community access sources are bibliographic and scoped only.
- Preserved prior source revisions, did not import real language corpora, did not change shared routing, and did not transfer to desktop.


## 1.0.0 — 14 November 2026: Scoped language scientific-content edition

- Adds 15 substantial chapters and 10 investigations to total 65 chapters and 40 investigations.
- Adds 6 published bibliographic source cards, 46 authored concept/method/claim/casebook records, and 50 typed evidence relations.
- Adds bounded NFA, quantifier scope, weighted segment alignment, ordered sound change, character n-gram and community-rights accession methods.
- Preserves prior v0.2 original records and archived tests, other eight nodes, and Common Contract routing unchanged.
- Original Unicode18 normative tests and authentic field corpora unavailable: independent scientific and live-model acceptance **not** claimed.
