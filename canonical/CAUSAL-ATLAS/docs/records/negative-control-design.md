# Negative controls need a causal rationale

**Record:** `causal-atlas:negative-control-design`  
**Kind:** method  
**Evidence:** primary_portion_checked_by_assistant

A negative-control exposure or outcome should not have the causal relation being tested but should be informative about relevant bias pathways. An arbitrary unrelated variable is not automatically an effective control.

## Assumptions



## Applicability

- Declared target and data-generating conditions only.

## Limitations

- A control finding can flag a problem without identifying its cause or magnitude.
