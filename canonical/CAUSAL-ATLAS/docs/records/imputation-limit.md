# An imputed value is not an observed value

**Record:** `causal-atlas:imputation-limit`  
**Kind:** concept  
**Evidence:** primary_portion_checked_by_assistant

Multiple imputation represents uncertainty through multiple completed datasets and an appropriate combining procedure. Single fills and incompatible models may misrepresent both uncertainty and association.

## Assumptions



## Applicability

- Declared target and data-generating conditions only.

## Limitations

- No imputation engine or multiple-imputation variance estimator is implemented.
