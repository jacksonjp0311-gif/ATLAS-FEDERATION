# History-dependent exposure needs history-dependent methods

**Record:** `causal-atlas:longitudinal-warning`  
**Kind:** concept  
**Evidence:** primary_portion_checked_by_assistant

Time-varying covariates affected by earlier exposure can create problems for ordinary adjustment. The cited marginal-structural approach concerns exposure histories and their assumptions, not repeated application of a point-exposure formula.
