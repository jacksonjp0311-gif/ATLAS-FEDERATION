# Exact population identities are not sample guarantees

**Record:** `causal-atlas:sample-versus-population`  
**Kind:** concept  
**Evidence:** authored_mathematical_teaching_material

The population comparison integrates over a fully supplied synthetic distribution. The sample routine averages supplied observations and predictions. Even with appropriate nuisance values, sampling variation can separate methods; exact agreement in a deliberately balanced example is not a general sample theorem.
