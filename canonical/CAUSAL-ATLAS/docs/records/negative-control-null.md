# A quiet control does not certify the main result

**Record:** `causal-atlas:negative-control-null`  
**Kind:** concept  
**Evidence:** primary_portion_checked_by_assistant

A null negative-control finding can be uninformative when the control does not share the relevant bias mechanism or has little sensitivity. Do not convert one null diagnostic into universal absence of bias.

## Assumptions



## Applicability

- Declared target and data-generating conditions only.

## Limitations

- No power analysis or bias calibration is supplied.
