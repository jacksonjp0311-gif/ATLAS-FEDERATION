# Double robustness is conditional

**Record:** `causal-atlas:double-robust-limits`  
**Kind:** concept  
**Evidence:** primary_portion_checked_by_assistant

With causal identification and regularity conditions, consistency can hold when one nuisance-model family is correctly specified. Neither unmeasured confounding nor two misspecified families are repaired merely by choosing AIPW.
