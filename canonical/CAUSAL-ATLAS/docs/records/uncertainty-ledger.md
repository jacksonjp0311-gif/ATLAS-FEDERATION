# Track different kinds of uncertainty separately

**Record:** `causal-atlas:uncertainty-ledger`  
**Kind:** method  
**Evidence:** authored_mathematical_teaching_material

The investigation workbook separates data error, sampling variability, identification assumptions, model specification and target transport. A narrow sampling interval cannot quantify an unmeasured-confounding mechanism that was excluded from the analysis. The local estimator returns no confidence interval rather than inventing one.

## Assumptions

- The example’s stated mechanism or the protocol’s declared scope.

## Applicability

- Declared target and data-generating conditions only.

## Limitations

- Not independent empirical validation or a general identification result.
