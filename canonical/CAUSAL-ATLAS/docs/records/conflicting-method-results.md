# Disagreement should trigger investigation

**Record:** `causal-atlas:conflicting-method-results`  
**Kind:** method  
**Evidence:** authored_mathematical_teaching_material

When crude, normalized IPW, unnormalized IPW and AIPW differ, retain every output and its target and model assumptions. In a synthetic example two wrong nuisance families give -0.26 when the known target is +0.10. Selecting the most attractive estimate would conceal the failure.

## Applicability
