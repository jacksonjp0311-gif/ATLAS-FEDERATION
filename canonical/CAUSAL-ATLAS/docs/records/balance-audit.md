# Balance is a diagnostic, not proof of exchangeability

**Record:** `causal-atlas:balance-audit`  
**Kind:** method  
**Evidence:** primary_portion_checked_by_assistant

Inspect weighted baseline means and distributional features rather than treatment-prediction accuracy alone. Good measured balance cannot establish the absence of unmeasured common causes.

## Assumptions



## Applicability

- Declared target and data-generating conditions only.

## Limitations

- This toolkit reports only one declared covariate mean, not a complete balance assessment.
