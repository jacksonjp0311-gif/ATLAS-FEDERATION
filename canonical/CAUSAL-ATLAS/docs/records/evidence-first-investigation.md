# A process-change investigation has decision checkpoints

**Record:** `causal-atlas:evidence-first-investigation`  
**Kind:** method  
**Evidence:** authored_mathematical_teaching_material

Before attributing a defect-rate shift to a new process, document the intervention, job population, assignment mechanism, chronology, material mix, outcome recording and competing changes. The proposed workflow yields an evidence ledger and scoped conclusion, not a binary causal certificate.

## Limitations
