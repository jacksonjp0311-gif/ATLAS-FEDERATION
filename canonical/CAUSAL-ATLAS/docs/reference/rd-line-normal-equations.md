# Exact weighted two-by-two boundary fit

With positive kernel weights and two distinct scaled scores, the intercept follows from weighted normal equations; the reported jump subtracts left from right intercept.

## Equation

`a=(S2*T0-S1*T1)/(S0*S2-S1^2)`

## Assumptions and limits

- Exact arithmetic for bounded inputs is not error-free causal inference.
