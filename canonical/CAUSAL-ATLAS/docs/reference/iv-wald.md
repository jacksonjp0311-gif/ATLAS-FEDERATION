# Binary-instrument Wald ratio

The outcome mean contrast divided by the treatment-participation contrast is the binary ratio; its causal interpretation requires the stated identification assumptions.

## Equation

`theta_W = (E[Y|Z=1]-E[Y|Z=0]) / (E[D|Z=1]-E[D|Z=0])`

## Assumptions and limits

- Undefined at a zero denominator; no sample uncertainty computed by the local tool.
