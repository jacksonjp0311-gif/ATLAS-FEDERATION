# 51. Minimum sufficient chemistry for technological continuity

**Field:** Research program · **Version:** v0.2.0 · **Scientific status:** original educational reference; cited publisher portions inspected, original publisher bytes not archived. No real experiment.

## Principles and physical scope

If hardware, industrial supply chains and model providers disappear, a society rebuilding computing capability needs a linked knowledge chain. Chemical conservation and thermodynamics support material selection; electrochemistry supports storage and sensing; crystal and interface chemistry support transistors; transport and contamination science support reliability; measurement and data provenance support reproducible discovery. These foundations are necessary but not sufficient for rebuilding AI processors. They connect to the separate Mathematics/Physics, Electronics/Semiconductors, Manufacturing, Computer Architecture, Compiler and Neural Architecture Atlases in the living priority overlay. A knowledge archive must not claim that a handful of formulas can replace clean rooms, scientific instruments, experienced people or primary experimental data.

## Equation and definitions

`Technological reconstruction dependency: exact SI → mass/charge/energy conservation → material properties → characterization and fabrication science → computing hardware → software toolchains → advanced AI. Each link has an independent evidence and safety gate.`

Inputs and reference states must be declared. The algebra alone cannot establish source data validity, experimental behavior or physical safety.

## Derivation and why the relationship holds

The dependency graph shows where expertise and data are still missing. Exact SI constants do not establish measured thermochemical values; reaction conservation does not guarantee a feasible or safe industrial process; a bulk semiconductor carrier equation does not provide a working transistor; and a compiler alone does not manufacture a CPU. A durable archive should define the minimum reconstructible explanation for each node, plus source rights, numerical tests, counterexamples and restoration procedures. This edition adds scoped methods but leaves genuine materials datasets, controlled experimental validation and independent review open.

## Reconstruct without external AI

Use only the offline manual to reconstruct the mole, energy, a charge-balanced reaction, a thermal equilibrium estimate, a diffusion conservation check, and a semiconductor carrier calculation. Then identify which additional knowledge would be needed to fabricate even a simple transistor. The exercise measures understanding of dependencies, not practical chip fabrication.

These examples are invented, not laboratory readings. Reproduce them by hand and compare only within the declared model domain.

## Failure case and missing evidence

Chemistry v0.2 remains below scoped content v1.0 acceptance. Nothing here certifies chemical handling, safe synthesis, electronic-device yield, data rights, experimental accuracy or real AI performance. All 30 scientific domains precede the new Bitwork router.
