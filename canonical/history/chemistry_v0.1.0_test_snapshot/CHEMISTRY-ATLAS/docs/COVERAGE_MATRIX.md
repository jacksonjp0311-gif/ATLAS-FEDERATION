## Technological continuity outcomes

- **Chemistry to Materials:** electronic structure, defects, corrosion, phase stability, polymers and process chemistry.
- **Chemistry to Energy:** electrochemical potentials and charge, thermodynamics, chemical heat, conversion and storage.
- **Chemistry to Electronics:** carrier concentration/mobility, semiconductor impurities, oxide dielectrics and interface quality.
- **Chemistry to AI research:** persistent compound identities, data provenance, scientific validation, leakage controls.
- **Chemistry to scientific tools:** exact stoichiometric algebra, quantitative models, spectroscopy concepts, metrology and calibration.

**Deferred:** safe actual chemical synthesis/industrial process specifications, original rights-cleared source replicas, genuine training or evaluation of chemical ML models, advanced reaction mechanism networks, modern transistor manufacturing and hardware benchmarking. **No new router** has been created.
