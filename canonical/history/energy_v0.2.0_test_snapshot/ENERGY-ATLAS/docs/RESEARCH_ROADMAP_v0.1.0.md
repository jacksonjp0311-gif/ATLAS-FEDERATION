# Energy Atlas: roadmap from foundation to scoped v1.0

**Acceptance objective:** a reconstructible human scientific and engineering reference capable of teaching energy first principles, defining the correct measurements and models, and clearly separating what is established from what remains unknown. It will not claim complete civilization rebuilding or permission to work on hazardous equipment.

## Next research content

- Thermodynamics: rigorous control-volume open-system equations, entropy and exergy balances, psychrometrics, real-fluid properties and calibration.
- Grid science: balanced/unbalanced AC power flow, per-unit systems, dynamic stability, electrical protection concepts and uncertainty in renewable forecast profiles, with no unsafe operational instruction.
- Storage: electrochemical equivalent circuits, self-discharge and thermal aging, state estimation, BMS protections, pumped hydro, compressed gas and thermochemical options.
- Generation: detailed PV diode modelling and reference weather data, measured wind resource distributions, turbine generator efficiency, hydro flow/head surveys and geothermal enthalpy sources.
- Energy-aware computation: complete hardware energy measurement protocol, data movement and cooling accounting; fair equal-quality workloads for future Bitwork evaluations (no new router before domain 30).
- Scientific reproduction: acquire original rights-cleared primary-source bytes and raw data, instrument calibration and benchmark-protocol reports; independently reproduce and review representative claims before scoped v1.

## Release gates

1. Source/rights closure and durable original-source acquisition, preserving source editions.
2. More sophisticated energy-model methods and counterexamples, including model-invalid ranges.
3. Independent numerical reproductions and, where ethically/legal/safe, separate experimental data comparison.
4. Peer or qualified engineering review on claims used for actual construction.
5. Preserve all previous nine node bytes and source revisions; seal distribution, extract, run complete tests and restore audit.

## Program order

Finish this Energy Atlas content toward scoped v1, then continue with Bioelectric and remaining roadmap domains. **Do not implement Bitwork routing until all thirty domain content libraries are built.** Integration and desktop transfer come afterward.
