# Energy Atlas — scoped v1 research and release gates

## Scientific content gates

1. Reconstruct mathematical foundations across thermodynamics, heat transfer, electrical machines, renewable and conventional generation, storage, resilience and metrology.
2. Publish exact source-linked versioned records with proof/counterexample scope, SI units and author-source distinction; correct historical records only by new revisions.
3. Include physically informative, tested reference algorithms with deliberately unsafe/out-of-scope actions excluded.
4. Provide enough standalone offline documentation for readers to derive the mathematics without proprietary models.
5. Obtain properly licensed authentic original scientific sources (not merely names and URLs); document 0-of-N when unavailable.

## Evidence and real-world acceptance gates (separate)

- Calibrated experimental measurements and uncertainty models, verified physical constants edition, source-derived material properties.
- Independently reviewed safety protocols and compliance for real BESS, wiring, islanding, protection, combustible fuels, potable-water supply and medical-critical systems.
- Tested islanded/grid-forming controls, storage dynamics, power-flow, protective devices and black-start under appropriately controlled site conditions.
- Outage simulation across representative and correlated renewable/weather/load distributions, including component faults and recovery.
- Verified field consequences and independent peer review; no probability or safety claims from an authored scenario.
- Verified source rights, original source bytes with hashes, redundant offline storage, migration and restore drills.
