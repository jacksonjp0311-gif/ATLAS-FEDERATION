# Energy Atlas v0.2.0 — roadmap to scoped content v1.0

**Priority 1: Original source survival.** Acquire exact rights-cleared NIST/CODATA, BIPM, DOE, EIA and applicable public-health source files with hashes, release date, license and separate review. Do not relabel third-party records as locally authentic without bytes.

**Priority 2: Physical service precision.** Expand water pump flow/head curves, calorimetry, heat-pump part-load maps, battery ageing and temperature dependence, PV weather/shading and realistic electrical power/energy constraints, with uncertainty and independent reference examples.

**Priority 3: Grid reliability.** Develop bounded AC power flow, component outages, islanding controls, black-start semantics, frequency/voltage/transient checks, commissioning and appropriate standards orientation. Do not turn educational code into a real controller or bypass certified equipment/protection.

**Priority 4: Real-world evidence.** Use legally acquired calibrated data, record raw transport bytes and sensor metadata, compare against a held-out physical/equipment benchmark, and preserve failures and alternative models.

**Priority 5: Knowledge-survival restoration.** Audit every chapter for independent reconstructibility, source rights, reproducible tests and readable offline format. Test archive restoration on different systems when possible.

**Global sequence:** all 30 domain libraries, then Bitwork router, then integrated evaluation and desktop transfer. Energy v0.2 is active; Bioelectric is queued, not started.
