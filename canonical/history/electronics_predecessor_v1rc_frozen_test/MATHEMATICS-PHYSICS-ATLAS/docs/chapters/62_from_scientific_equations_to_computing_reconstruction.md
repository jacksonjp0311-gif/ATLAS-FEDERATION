# 62. From scientific equations to reconstructible computational systems

## An executable, layered dependency chain

ATLAS FORGE preserves a *reconstructible* mathematics-to-hardware-to-AI chain, not a standalone emergency response manual. The correct handoff order is (1) definitions of number, algebra and dimensional consistency; (2) calculus, weak or finite-volume conservation and robust numerical solvers; (3) physical quantities measured with traceable instruments; (4) materials and chemical processes that alter carrier behavior; (5) semiconductor electrostatics and transport with reliable contact and interface models; (6) fabrication, characterization and electrical measurement; (7) transistor circuits, digital logic and microarchitecture; (8) compilers, software and reproducible numerical libraries; and (9) trained/inferred AI with explicit evaluation and persistent evidence.

A step is *reconstructible* only if it includes assumptions, source lineage, actionable algorithms, failure and uncertainty conditions, reference test vectors and practical validation steps. A readable paper by itself is not a fabrication process, and a calibrated transistor requires material and process information absent from this mathematics reference.

## What this edition can reproduce offline

With Python 3.10+ standard library: rational/finite arithmetic, selected linear-algebra and FFT references, bounded ODE/PDE models, two-dimensional conservative electrostatics, nonlinear Poisson–Boltzmann equilibrium, Scharfetter–Gummel edge flux, and a steady 1D two-carrier coupled Poisson/continuity solver with residual reporting. The 56 prior chapters and all historical exact revisions are retained. A deterministic test runner verifies historical cases and the new reference functions. The full Atlas federation can discover, fetch and context-assemble qualified exact-revision records using the unchanged common schema without implementing the later Bitwork router.

## What remains beyond this science layer

A device-level instrument-calibrated semiconductor library must specify dopant ionization, material band structure and intrinsic carrier density, temperature-dependent mobility, recombination mechanisms, contact boundary conditions, heterointerfaces, interface state charge, coupling to heat and mechanical tolerances, measured calibration datasets, and confidence intervals. The present solver lacks generation/recombination and intrinsic charge-coupled contact models. Semiconductor *manufacturing* further requires crystallography, precursor purity and handling, photolithography masks and optics, etch/deposition physics, metrology, defect yield, packaging, test benches and reproducible process windows. No such fabrication system was constructed or experimentally evaluated in this Atlas release.

## Acceptance separation

**Software-content check:** mathematical derivations, runnable bounded code, deterministic tests, independent dense-oracle comparisons, exact-revision Atlas records and archive restore. **Primary-source check:** licensed official original publisher files with byte hashes and date-locked provenance; not yet satisfied. **Scientific-model check:** independent domain experts evaluate assumptions, theoretical limits and calibrations; not yet satisfied. **Experimental check:** real laboratories compare predictions with measured devices; not performed. The contents may be called a software research reference but cannot honestly be described as a scientifically accepted or independently validated semiconductor reconstruction program.
