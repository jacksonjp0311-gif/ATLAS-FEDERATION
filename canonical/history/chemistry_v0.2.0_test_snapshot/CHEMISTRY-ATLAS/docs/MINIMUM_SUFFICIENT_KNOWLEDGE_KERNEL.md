# CHEMISTRY ATLAS — Minimum Sufficient Technological Knowledge Kernel

**Purpose:** preserve sufficient knowledge to reconstruct modern science and continue developing computation and advanced AI. This is not a general-purpose emergency-preparedness or survivalist manual. The content is a minimum educational reference, not a claim that these chapters alone could rebuild a chip fabrication facility.

## Dependency structure and the core problem

Modern AI is stored not only in weights and source code but in the physical systems needed to execute and improve them. Microelectronics rests on the chemical and physical properties of matter. Chemical theory supports silicon and insulating oxides, copper and other interconnects, conductive and dielectric materials, high-purity substrates, battery and power-system materials, environmental compatibility and reliable measurements. Those dependencies make chemistry a priority even when the eventual goal is software intelligence.

The reference is designed to protect the *reasoning chain*, not just a large set of facts. Every major claim should have a physical definition, an equation, an explicit input or evidence source, a reconstructible algorithm, a counterexample, a failure domain and an appropriate rights statement. An AI model consuming the Atlas should not promote a retrieved explanation into an unverified action or scientific certainty.

## Six knowledge strata

1. **Quantity and identity:** SI units, atomic identity, isotope and molar-mass uncertainty; provenance and evidence that the sample is what it claims to be.
2. **Conservation:** exact atom and charge bookkeeping, reaction coefficient balance, mass and energy accounting, phase and temperature context.
3. **Thermodynamics and kinetics:** equilibrium via dimensionless activities, Gibbs energy, Arrhenius and transport models, and their clear applicability boundaries.
4. **Materials:** crystal order, defects, phase diagrams, polymers, corrosion, purity, contaminants, spectroscopy and physical characterization.
5. **Electronic components:** semiconductors and carriers, oxide interfaces, diffusion, conductive/insulating properties, energy storage and physical lifetime.
6. **AI and software continuity:** structural compound representations, versioned scientific datasets, reproducibility, leakage controls and source-closed retrieval that can support future training or research.

The transition from layers 1–6 is a *dependency graph*, not a guarantee of manufacturing sufficiency. Electronics, computer architecture, manufacturing tooling, operating systems, compilers and machine learning require separate Atlases. The complete scientific program can prioritize them dynamically without altering historical validated releases.

## What can be reconstructed now

Using only offline plain text and Python: parse a restricted neutral formula; derive an integer atom-balanced reaction with exact rational linear algebra; compute molar quantities from caller-supplied masses; calculate concentration changes; evaluate thermodynamic ΔG and K under declared standard states; calculate first-order kinetics and Arrhenius ratios; estimate reversible electrochemical potentials; and evaluate idealized semiconductor conductivity and diffusion lengths. Each result must preserve the distinction between arithmetic correctness and a validated chemical experiment.

## What must *not* be treated as solved

Original publisher-file archival; exact rights for OpenStax Chemistry 2e edition and other licensed works; experimental thermochemical values and uncertainties; arbitrary ionic/redox balance and stereochemistry; real chemical reaction networks; safe industrial synthesis and physical procedures; semiconductor process purity/yield; independent peer review; an operational chemical AI; and the reconstruction of a processor from chemistry alone. The present sources are **14 bibliographic/scoped references** with **zero original publisher files obtained**.

## The acceptance rule and version order

First complete a modest but truthful v0.1 kernel in Chemistry, then deepen to scoped content v1. Preserve earlier nodes and their original record hashes. Apply the active technological-continuity priority order: Chemistry → Mathematics/Physics → Electronics/Semiconductors → Manufacturing → Computer Architecture, with Neural Architectures and the rest of the original 30 workstreams maintained in parallel planning priority. Do not build Bitwork routing until the full domain-content phase is complete, and do not transfer a prematurely assembled incomplete system to the user's desktop.
