# Energy Atlas v1 scientific acceptance matrix

## Eligible scoped CONTENT v1.0.0 gates

- 64 human-readable scientific chapters with terms, equations, preconditions and counterexamples
- 51 separate constructed investigations from v0.1–v1, all visibly synthetic
- Exact-revision source and relationship closure; no prior Energy record/relation lost
- Numerical oracle and full ten-node engineering regression passing from a sealed ZIP
- Rights/primary-source gaps retained instead of inventing source bytes
- Complete offline human guide and reconstructible source code without any cloud model

## Scientific/physical ACCEPTANCE (HOLD)

- Independent technical peer review of equations, precision and omitted physics
- Actual site meteorology, measured electrical load and calibrated equipment data
- Grid-forming/islanding/protection testing by qualified engineers under current codes
- Certified battery, generator, and fire/CO safety evidence including NFPA 855 requirements
- Water-quality and treatment evidence from appropriate public health authority; heated water is not necessarily potable
- Statistically defensible, correlated reliability scenarios and independent validation
- Rights-cleared authentic primary-source files with immutable provenance and SHA-256
- Verified physical implementation, geographic backups and no-network recovery drill on separate machine

These open gates must remain HOLD even when the software checks pass.
