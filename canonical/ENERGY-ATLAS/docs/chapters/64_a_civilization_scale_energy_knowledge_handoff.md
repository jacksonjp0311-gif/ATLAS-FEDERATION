# 64. A civilization-scale energy knowledge handoff

**Domain:** Knowledge survival. **Release:** Energy Atlas 1.0.0 scoped content.
**Evidence:** Original author-assisted synthesis with selected official publisher webpage citations. No physical system, original research data, or site measurement verified.

## Definition, goal and physical boundary

The end state of Energy Atlas is an independently readable and reconstructible scientific reference, not a particular generator design. Future people should understand work, heat and electricity from units and derivations; test mathematical models in a small offline interpreter; and clearly identify where specialist engineering, materials, grid safety and primary source evidence are missing. A citation is not a backup. Local rights-cleared original files, hashed provenance, unit definitions, experimental uncertainty and machine-readable interfaces must survive alongside human explanations. The thirty-domain library can later connect energy to Materials, Measurement, Control, Computation, Causal and Agent knowledge, but routing must preserve qualifications rather than creating unwarranted certainty.

## Formula, units and preconditions

`Evidence chain: original bytes + rights + source/version + transformation logs + input units + model assumptions + independent verification + limitations → qualified scientific claim. Missing links must remain explicitly missing.`

Power (W or kW) and energy (J or kWh) must never be interchanged without time integration and unit conversion. Every equation is conditional on the specified physical or mathematical model; exact arithmetic does not verify observed inputs.

## Derivation and reasoning

A clean restoration exercise starts with a frozen release inventory and plain-text manual, verifies source and relationship hashes, executes illustrative tests without internet, and reproduces a sample hand calculation. A hash verifies consistency against a trusted retained reference; it does not authenticate a publisher or certify a physical experiment. Evaluation should distinguish source-tree tests, extracted-archive replay, independent numerical oracle, expert scientific review and actual equipment trials. Passing the first three is valuable software evidence, not completion of the last two.

## Reconstruct by hand and compare against code

Ask a hypothetical reader to reproduce 1 kWh=3.6 MJ, a water-lifting energy calculation, a thermal envelope budget, and a three-interval battery reserve case, using only the offline manual and plain Python. Then require a separate explanation of why none certifies equipment safety or potable water. The exercise tests reconstructibility, not real-world resilience.

Every numerical quantity in this exercise is an authored, deliberately stipulated example—not an observation of real devices. The offline `energy_atlas.survival_v1` module implements the relevant bounded models, and tests cover refusals as well as positive cases.

## Failure modes, alternative hypotheses and missing evidence

Primary-source acquisition remains unfinished. The NIST ASCII constants file could not be downloaded in this environment, and restricted NFPA standards must not be falsely mirrored. Scientific and cultural scope is incomplete, and no independent disaster-recovery facility was tested.

## Life-safety and professional boundary

The next work should prioritize legal primary source acquisition, expert methodological review, real-world validated calibration data and portable distribution; all thirty domain nodes must be built before Bitwork routing or desktop installation.
