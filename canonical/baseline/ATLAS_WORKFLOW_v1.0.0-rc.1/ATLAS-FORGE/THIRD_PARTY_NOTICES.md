# Rights and imported reference nodes

This package incorporates the user's original Atlas reference metadata at the user's request. Per-source rights and attribution remain in every imported source record. Linked scientific papers, datasets and standards are not mirrored or relicensed. No raw external experimental data or upstream research PDFs are included.

Signal Atlas's published MIT license is retained alongside its node. Cymatics Atlas's project license and research-rights notice are retained alongside its node. These do not transfer rights in linked third-party publications. Measurement's source is a navigation card, not a licensed copy of NIST technical guidance.

Forge's own public licensing decision remains pending as in v0.1.0. This local integration does not change it. Review [LICENSE_DECISIONS.md](LICENSE_DECISIONS.md) before public publication. Do not treat the availability of a source link as redistribution permission.
