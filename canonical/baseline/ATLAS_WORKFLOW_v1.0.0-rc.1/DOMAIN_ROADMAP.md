## No duplicate knowledge silos

Grok’s 20 domains complement the original ten. Closely related domains must share stable concept and source references. Multi-agent coordination belongs near Agent Atlas; energy systems modeling near Energy Atlas; simulation and digital twins near Control Atlas. Separate a new node only when its ownership and scope are clear.

## Measurement v1 blockers

Claim-level source verification, authentic licensed calibration examples, independent review, real Bitwork-to-context evaluation, held-out benchmark, and supported-platform checks remain open. No later domain starts before acceptance.
