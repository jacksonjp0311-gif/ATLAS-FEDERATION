# Expanded uncertainty

**Record:** `expanded-uncertainty` · **Type:** equation · **Origin:** source_reported

Expanded uncertainty multiplies combined standard uncertainty by a chosen coverage factor.

## Declared relation

```text
U = k u_c
```

## Source and inspected portion

[Expanded uncertainty](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297/nist-tn-1297-6-expanded-uncertainty) · §6.1

## Assumptions

- Coverage factor k is explicit and justified.

## Limitations

- Multiplication alone does not establish a coverage probability.
