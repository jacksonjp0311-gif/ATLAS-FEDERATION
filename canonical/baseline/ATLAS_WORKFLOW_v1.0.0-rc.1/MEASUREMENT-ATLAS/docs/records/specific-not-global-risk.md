# Specific risk is not global process risk

**Record:** `specific-not-global-risk` · **Type:** concept · **Origin:** source_reported

An item-specific probability does not determine process-wide error rates without process and measurement distributions.

## Source and inspected portion

[Measurement uncertainty in conformity assessment](https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/2071204/JCGM_106_2012_E.pdf/fe9537d2-e7d7-e146-5abb-2649c3450b25) · §9.3.2–9.4.3

## Assumptions

- The item probability is conditional on its measured information.

## Limitations

- The new tool reports global consumer and producer risks as unavailable.

Assistant source-portion inspection or explicit local derivation; no independent scientific review or physical certification.
