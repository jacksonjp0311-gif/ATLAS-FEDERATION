# Coverage depends on uncertainty-estimate reliability

**Record:** `coverage-degrees-of-freedom` · **Type:** concept · **Origin:** source_reported

A coverage factor intended to approximate a specified probability must account for distributional conditions and the reliability of the estimated uncertainty.

## Source and inspected portion

[Coverage factors and effective degrees of freedom](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297/nist-tn-1297-appendix-b-coverage-factors) · B.1–B.2

## Assumptions

- The conventional approximation and its distributional conditions apply.

## Limitations

- The number k=2 is not a universal 95-percent guarantee.
