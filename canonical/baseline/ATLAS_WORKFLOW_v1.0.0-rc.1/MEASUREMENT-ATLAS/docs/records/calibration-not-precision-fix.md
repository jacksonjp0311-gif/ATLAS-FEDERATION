# Calibration does not inherently improve precision

**Record:** `calibration-not-precision-fix` · **Type:** concept · **Origin:** source_reported

Bias correction through calibration is not inherently a reduction of replicate scatter; assess performance in the intended environment.

## Source and inspected portion

[Calibration failure modes](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section3/mpc364.htm) · §2.3.6.4, Lack of precision

## Assumptions

- The distinction between bias and dispersion is retained.

## Limitations

- A smaller correction after adjustment does not prove better repeatability.
