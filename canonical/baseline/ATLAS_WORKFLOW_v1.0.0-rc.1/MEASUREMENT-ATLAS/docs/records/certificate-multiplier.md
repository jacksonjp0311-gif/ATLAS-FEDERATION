# Convert a reported uncertainty multiplier

**Record:** `certificate-multiplier` · **Type:** method · **Origin:** source_reported

Divide an uncertainty stated as a multiple of a standard deviation by its stated multiplier.

## Declared relation

```text
u = U/k
```

## Source and inspected portion

[Type B evaluation](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297/nist-tn-1297-4-type-b-evaluation-standard-uncertainty) · §4.2

## Assumptions

- The report supplies the multiplier and meaning of U.

## Limitations

- A missing multiplier cannot be guessed.

Assistant source-portion inspection or explicit local derivation; no independent scientific review or physical replication.
