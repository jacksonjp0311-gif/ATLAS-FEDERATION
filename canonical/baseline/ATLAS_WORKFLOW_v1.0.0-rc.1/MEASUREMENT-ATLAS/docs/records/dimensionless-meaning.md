# Dimension one does not remove quantity meaning

**Record:** `dimensionless-meaning` · **Type:** concept · **Origin:** source_reported

Ratios and counts with unit one still need their quantity meaning and reference basis identified.

## Source and inspected portion

[SI quantities, dimensions and units](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-330/sp-330-section-2) · §2.3.3, dimension-one discussion

## Assumptions

- The numerator, denominator or counted entity is defined.

## Limitations

- A fraction, a radian measure and a count are not interchangeable context.
