# Measurement may disturb the target

**Record:** `measurement-intervention` · **Type:** concept · **Origin:** source_reported

Instrument interaction can change the quantity being observed. A justified correction may be needed to estimate the originally intended quantity.

## Source and inspected portion

[Measurand and measurement intervention](https://jcgm.bipm.org/vim/en/2.3.html) · 2.3, note 3

## Assumptions

- The interaction has a defensible measurement model.

## Limitations

- The record does not provide a universal correction for arbitrary instruments.

Assistant source-portion inspection or explicit local derivation; no independent scientific review or physical replication.
