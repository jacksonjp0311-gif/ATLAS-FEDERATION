# Repeatability conditions

**Record:** `repeatability-conditions` · **Type:** concept · **Origin:** source_reported

A repeatability study holds the procedure, operators, system, operating conditions and location fixed over a short period.

## Source and inspected portion

[Repeatability conditions](https://jcgm.bipm.org/vim/en/2.20.html) · 2.20, definition and note 1

## Assumptions

- The scope of what remains unchanged is documented.

## Limitations
