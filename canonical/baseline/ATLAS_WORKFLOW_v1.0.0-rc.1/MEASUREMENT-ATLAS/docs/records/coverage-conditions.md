# Coverage factor is not a universal probability

**Record:** `coverage-conditions` · **Type:** concept · **Origin:** source_reported

A factor of two corresponds to approximately 95 percent coverage under the stated normal-distribution and negligible-uncertainty conditions.

## Source and inspected portion

[Expanded uncertainty](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297/nist-tn-1297-6-expanded-uncertainty) · §6.2–6.3

## Assumptions

- Normal approximation applies and uncertainty in u_c is negligible for that interpretation.

## Limitations

- Do not label every k=2 interval as 95 percent.
