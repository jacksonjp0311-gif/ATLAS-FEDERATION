# Tolerance and uncertainty answer different questions

**Record:** `tolerance-not-uncertainty` · **Type:** concept · **Origin:** source_reported

Specification limits describe permissible values; uncertainty describes knowledge about a value.

## Source and inspected portion

[Measurement uncertainty in conformity assessment](https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/2071204/JCGM_106_2012_E.pdf/fe9537d2-e7d7-e146-5abb-2649c3450b25) · §3.3.4–3.3.9 and §7.1

## Assumptions

- One scalar property and explicit limits.

## Limitations

- Neither a tolerance nor a resolution automatically supplies a standard uncertainty.

Assistant source-portion inspection or explicit local derivation; no independent scientific review or physical certification.
