# Dimensions alone do not identify a quantity

**Record:** `dimensions-not-kind` · **Type:** concept · **Origin:** source_reported

Different quantity kinds can have the same dimension or unit. Dimensional agreement is necessary for many calculations, but is not a complete semantic check.

## Source and inspected portion

[SI quantities, dimensions and units](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-330/sp-330-section-2) · §§2.3.3–2.3.4

## Assumptions

- Use the declared SI dimensional convention.

## Limitations

- Energy and torque must not be treated as interchangeable merely because their dimensions match.
