# Calibration results have instrument and time scope

**Record:** `certificate-time-instrument-scope` · **Type:** concept · **Origin:** source_reported

NIST calibration results apply to the specified instrument or standard at its test time unless the report states otherwise.

## Source and inspected portion

[NIST calibration policies: scope and reporting](https://www.nist.gov/calibrations/policies) · Reports of Calibration/Test Results

## Assumptions

- Use the conditions and scope of the particular report.

## Limitations

- No perpetual validity or automatic next-calibration date follows.

Assistant source-portion inspection or explicit local derivation; no independent scientific review or physical certification.
