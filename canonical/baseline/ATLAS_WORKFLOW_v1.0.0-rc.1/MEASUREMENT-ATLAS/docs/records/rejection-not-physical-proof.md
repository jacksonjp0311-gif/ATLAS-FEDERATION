# Not accepted is not proved nonconforming

**Record:** `rejection-not-physical-proof` · **Type:** concept · **Origin:** source_reported

An acceptance decision can be wrong; a rejected item may still meet its specification.

## Source and inspected portion

[Measurement uncertainty in conformity assessment](https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/2071204/JCGM_106_2012_E.pdf/fe9537d2-e7d7-e146-5abb-2649c3450b25) · §9.3–9.4

## Assumptions

- Decision outcomes and true conformity remain separate events.

## Limitations

- An output label is not a calibration certificate or proof of physical state.

Assistant source-portion inspection or explicit local derivation; no independent scientific review or physical certification.
