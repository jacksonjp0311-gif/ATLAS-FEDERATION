# Certificate completeness is not authenticity

**Record:** `certificate-structure-not-authenticity` · **Type:** concept · **Origin:** formal_derivation

A complete transcribed report is input for review; checking its fields does not verify its signatory, authenticity or the claimed traceability chain.

## Source and inspected portion

[Metrological traceability FAQ](https://www.nist.gov/metrology/metrological-traceability) · FAQ §5.2.1; local software-boundary inference

## Assumptions

- The structure check has no access to external identity or laboratory verification.

## Limitations

- Never label a successful field check as accreditation or traceability certification.

Assistant source-portion inspection or explicit local derivation; no independent scientific review or physical certification.
