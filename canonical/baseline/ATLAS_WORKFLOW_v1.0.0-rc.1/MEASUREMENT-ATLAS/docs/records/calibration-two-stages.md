# Calibration relates standards, indications and results

**Record:** `calibration-two-stages` · **Type:** concept · **Origin:** source_reported

Calibration establishes a relationship with standards and uncertainties, then uses that relationship to obtain results from indications.

## Source and inspected portion

[Calibration](https://jcgm.bipm.org/vim/en/2.39.html) · 2.39, definition and notes 1 and 3

## Assumptions

- Specified conditions and applicable standards are identified.

## Limitations

- A calibration curve without uncertainty information does not supply a complete result.

Assistant source-portion inspection or explicit local derivation; no independent scientific review or physical replication.
