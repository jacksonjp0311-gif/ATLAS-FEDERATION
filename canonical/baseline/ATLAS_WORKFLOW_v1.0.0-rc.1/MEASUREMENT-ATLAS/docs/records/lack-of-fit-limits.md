# Lack-of-fit testing is not complete model validation

**Record:** `lack-of-fit-limits` · **Type:** claim · **Origin:** source_reported

A lack-of-fit test assesses the specified mean-response function under assumptions. It does not replace residual examination or establish all aspects of model quality.

## Source and inspected portion

[Replicate-based lack-of-fit assessment](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmd/section4/pmd446.htm) · §4.4.4.6 opening paragraphs and interpretation

## Assumptions

- The replication structure and variance assumptions are appropriate.

## Limitations

- Failure to reject is not proof of correctness.
- A numerical F ratio alone does not validate traceability, constant variance, independence or drift.
