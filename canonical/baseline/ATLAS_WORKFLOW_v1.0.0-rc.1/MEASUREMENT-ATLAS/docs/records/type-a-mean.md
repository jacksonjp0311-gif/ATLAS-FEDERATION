# Uncertainty of a repeated-reading mean

**Record:** `type-a-mean` · **Type:** method · **Origin:** source_reported

For independent observations under the same conditions, estimate the mean uncertainty from their sample dispersion.

## Declared relation

```text
u(mean) = s/sqrt(n)
```

## Source and inspected portion

[Law of propagation of uncertainty](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297/nist-tn-1297-appendix-law-propagation-uncertainty) · A.4, equations A-4 and A-5

## Assumptions

- n >= 2 independent observations under the same measurement conditions.

## Limitations

- Autocorrelation and drift can invalidate this calculation.
