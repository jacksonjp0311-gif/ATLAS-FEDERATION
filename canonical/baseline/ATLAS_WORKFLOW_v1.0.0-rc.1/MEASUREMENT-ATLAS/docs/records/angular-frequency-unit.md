# Cyclic and angular frequency are different quantities

**Record:** `angular-frequency-unit` · **Type:** equation · **Origin:** source_reported

Frequency in hertz and angular frequency in radians per second differ by a factor of 2π, even though both can be expressed using inverse seconds.

## Declared relation

```text
omega = 2*pi*f
```

## Source and inspected portion

[SI quantities, dimensions and units](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-330/sp-330-section-2) · §2.3.4, final frequency paragraph

## Assumptions

- The signal has a defined cycle or angular rate.

## Limitations

- Do not relabel Hz as rad/s without conversion.
