# Precision requires stated measurement conditions

**Record:** `precision-conditions` · **Type:** concept · **Origin:** source_reported

Replicate dispersion characterizes precision under stated conditions. Standard deviation, variance or a coefficient of variation describes imprecision, not automatically accuracy.

## Source and inspected portion

[Measurement precision](https://jcgm.bipm.org/vim/en/2.15.html) · 2.15, definition and notes 1–4

## Assumptions

- Replicates and their operating conditions are identified.

## Limitations

- A small spread does not demonstrate absence of common bias.

Assistant source-portion inspection or explicit local derivation; no independent scientific review or physical replication.
