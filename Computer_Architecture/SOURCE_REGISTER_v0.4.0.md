Capture complete original publisher files only through lawful, licensed access. Record retrieval date, final transport URL, publication edition, MIME/magic, byte length, SHA-256, permission to redistribute, and page/equation number. Preserve precise differences between a source claim and ATLAS FORGE's own derivation, with human reviewer identification and critique. An official URL, webpage snippet, generated summary, or numerical test alone is NOT a source-original receipt.

AT8 is an original educational instruction set. **It is not RISC-V, MIT Beta, Hack, or an implementation of any of those standard architectures.** Those systems are cited to orient future independent research and design comparisons.
