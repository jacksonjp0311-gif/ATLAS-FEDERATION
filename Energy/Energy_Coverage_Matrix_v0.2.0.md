# Energy Atlas v0.2.0 — survival-service coverage and scientific gaps

**Status:** substantial scientific reference development, not safe field-construction instructions, independent engineering review, or final content v1.0.

| Domain | Reconstructible reference and methods | Executed offline verification | Missing authentic evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| SI energy and thermodynamics | Energy, exergy, heat, entropy, Carnot and conservation | Preserved v0.1 thermodynamic models | Field measurement, calibration and primary source originals |
| Heat-transfer/shelter | Conductive/convective/radiative heat and degree-hours | Lumped/steady teaching cases and v0.2 heat budget | Real building UA, airflow, moisture, health limits and safe thermal equipment |

| Resilience outcomes | Unserved kWh and worst of supplied scenarios | Time-series accounting and explicit shortage metric | Statistical weather/resource distribution, correlated failures and true outage risk |
| Metrology and preservation | Original derivations, SI and NIST source references, rights notices | Common Atlas record/source closure, historical bytes and Python tests | Authenticated raw source PDFs/data; independent expert review and offsite restores |

No amount of synthetic validation proves real system resilience. A device serving fractional rated kW may not operate at all. Do not treat this corpus as an electrical design or potable-water certification.

**Current original-source preservation debt:** 463 bibliographic cards (455 prior + 8 new) and zero authenticated local publisher original bytes under the strict audit. This is a substantial unresolved survival requirement, not permission to mirror proprietary safety standards.

**Survival acceptance roadmap:** primary-source accession → site-calibrated examples → independently reviewed physical/electrical safety → hardware-in-the-loop with authorized systems → scoped v1 acceptance. All 30 domain libraries precede Bitwork routing and desktop transfer.
