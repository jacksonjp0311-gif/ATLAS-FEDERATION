# ATLAS FORGE — Technological Continuity Directive v2.0.0

**Issued:** November 14, 2026. **Revises the prior emergency-survival editorial overlay.** This directive changes priorities, **not the canonical thirty original domain IDs, software, evidence or user desktop**.

## Mission

Preserve the **minimum sufficient, independently reconstructible scientific and computational knowledge** needed for people to regain and advance technological civilization, especially **advanced AI, local models, agent tools, semiconductors, software compilers, scientific instruments, manufacturing and energy**.

This is **not a prepper survival guide**. Water, medicine, food, sanitation and shelter matter, but belong in rigorous supporting science and engineering or targeted evidence modules. They should not displace the capability to build computers and intelligent software.

**Chemistry is first among unbuilt workstreams.** Mathematics/physics, semiconductor electronics, manufacturing and computing architecture follow. Neural architectures receive an **early parallel seed**, so AI knowledge isn't delayed until every industrial dependency is complete.

## Minimum viable technological reconstruction packet

Every priority domain gets the same interoperable core: definitions and measurement standards; prerequisite concepts; equations/derivations; verifiable algorithms and data formats; source origin and rights; offline executable examples; counterexamples, error detection and unknowns; and restoration instructions. State separately what has been mathematically shown, experimentally observed, independently reviewed and genuinely archived in original bytes.

**Early stop rule:** If work ends before all domains are complete, the released materials must form a runnable vertical slice from *matter and mathematics → semiconductors and circuits → a computer architecture → compilers/operating systems → an AI model/training/inference example → evaluation and further software improvement*. The first release can be a small but correct seed, not a 70-chapter book.

## Two parallel tracks without two roadmaps

**Physical bootstrap:** Chemistry, physics/math, Electronics, Manufacturing, Hardware, Energy and Materials. Preserve enough to build and repair instruments and computation hardware.

**Intelligence bootstrap:** Math, Compilers, OS, Software, Neural Architectures, Networks, Databases, Evaluation, Knowledge Graphs and Agents. Preserve enough to continue creating AI systems even before the physical industry is fully recovered.

The tracks cross-link through the existing Atlas record schema. **Do not implement the Bitwork router yet**; the original rule remains all domain content first, router second, integrated evaluation third, desktop transfer last. Prepare the structure for routing without building the router prematurely.

## Source fidelity and scientific evidence

The prior complete Energy checkpoint reported 466 bibliography/source cards and zero authenticated local original publisher files under the strict audit. That is an urgent archival limitation. Acquire exact rights-cleared primary texts/data and version-locked sources when possible; never relabel links as preserved original bytes or infer empirical AI/model improvements from synthetic tests. If high-value sources cannot be mirrored legally, retain bibliographic rights/status and durable original explanation without copying restricted material.
