## Correction to the earlier directive

The earlier prioritization was overly focused on emergency supplies, sanitation and shelter. Those belong in the knowledge system, but ATLAS FORGE is **not a prepper guide**. It is a modular, rights-aware, versioned, human-and-AI-usable scientific library, with chemistry at the top and an explicit path from materials and fundamental physics to hardware, software and advanced AI. The prior survival directive remains in history but this v2 proposal supersedes its *editorial order* if adopted.

## The bootstrap sequence (why this order)

**Chemistry → Mathematics → Physics/electronics → Semiconductor hardware → Manufacturing → Compilers and operating systems → Networks, databases and distributed systems → Neural architectures and reliable autonomous research.** Each dependency is an architectural emphasis, not an absolute one-way prerequisite: mathematics and physics are built in parallel with chemistry rather than waiting for an entire chemistry encyclopedia.

Chemistry bridges atomic and molecular behavior, fuels, materials, electrochemistry, biological processes and chip fabrication. Mathematics preserves the language of derivations and optimization. Physics and semiconductor knowledge give a path to actual computing hardware. The software toolchain and systems domains keep the ability to compile, run, verify, store and improve future AI. The advanced AI domains remain prominent rather than relegated to the end for “survival.”

## Coverage without turning into a prepper manual

Provide compact science-based applications **inside linked domain references**: water chemistry and filtration science in Chemistry/Materials; microbiology, bioelectricity and public health fundamentals in Bioelectric/Bioinformatics and Chemistry; agriculture/soil/material transport and food systems in Chemistry/Materials/Manufacturing; shelter thermodynamics and civil systems in Energy/Materials/Manufacturing. These are reproducible scientific and engineering references, not improvised field treatment directions. If future scope requires standalone water, agriculture, clinical or civil domains, propose them as separately versioned additions rather than silently deleting original node IDs.

## Minimum surviving knowledge kernel (required for EVERY priority domain)

A domain is not “seed ready” until it contains: **(1)** human-readable foundations and definitions, **(2)** symbolic mathematics or appropriate scientifically grounded mechanisms, **(3)** at least one reproducible algorithm/experiment/derived example, **(4)** a counterexample, assumptions and uncertainty, **(5)** source provenance and clear rights/byte-acquisition status, **(6)** machine-readable cross-domain records with exact revisions, **(7)** offline reconstruction instructions and independent tests where practical, and **(8)** a ledger of what has not yet been proven. Passing software tests is not independent experimental science.

**Time-pressure rule:** Establish this kernel in all high-priority missing domains first, before extensive polishing of a single subject. Prioritize authenticated rights-cleared original source bytes and a durable restore path in parallel. Detailed scoped v1 depth can follow for each subject. Retain the original complete domain list; do not treat a version label as evidence of full knowledge.
