## Original-file acquisition gate

For future lawful source acquisition, record publisher, complete document version, retrieval URL, retrieval date, PDF/text MIME identification, file SHA-256, byte count, license/redistribution permission, matching figure/equation/page, independent technical reviewer, and any correction or withdrawal. A DOI or PMID citation does not substitute for that receipt. Distinguish original paper's experimental evidence from our independent educational reimplementation.
