# Rights, provenance, and hardware limitation

The offline-printing companion is original supporting material for the canonical ATLAS FORGE program. It is not an independent Atlas federation or a replacement edition. Its scripts, teaching geometry and assessments were created for this reconstruction exercise. No third-party publisher document, font or firmware image is embedded.

The six Atlas knowledge links refer to exact revisions in Full Continuity v0.5.0, but those nodes have not received authenticated original publisher-document transport or independent scientific review in the recorded offline audit. No assumption is made that all contents of the larger canonical Atlas can be publicly redistributed; its rights review remains incomplete.

The printable part is a nonstructural educational fixture. It does not claim electrical performance, physical material certification, fit, strength, food/water contact safety, thermal resistance, regulatory approval or suitability for a hazardous environment. Every physical acceptance claim must come from actual independently inspected parts and appropriately calibrated measurements.
