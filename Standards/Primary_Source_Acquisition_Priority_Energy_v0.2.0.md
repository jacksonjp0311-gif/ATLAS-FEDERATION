# ENERGY ATLAS — Primary source acquisition and safety boundary v0.2

**Confirmed surviving bytes:** original ATLAS FORGE authorship, model source code, tests, versioned records, and reading chapters. **Missing authentic publisher files:** all 463 bibliographic references under the strict local audit. Zero claims of original paper/PDF accession were made.

## Prioritize legally reusable scientific originals

1. **BIPM SI Brochure, NIST CODATA (2022 adjustment / published 2025):** obtain publisher originals and exact version, rights, transport SHA and uncertainty provenance. Avoid inventing raw data files from copied constants.
2. **DOE energy resilience and microgrid basics:** capture exact official HTML/PDF with source-date/version, license, and preservation hash where permitted. An HTML conceptual overview is not the same as a technical grid interconnection standard.
3. **EPA battery-energy-storage incident guidance and NFPA 855 (2026):** separately preserve public summaries and authoritative current installation standards subject to licensing. NFPA 855 is copyrighted; no full clauses were mirrored in this edition.
4. **CDC generator CO and EPA/CDC emergency drinking water:** preserve public health guidance, update dates and local applicability; energy estimates cannot certify safe fuel use or water quality.
5. **Real field source packs:** obtain consent/rights and traceable meter calibration, equipment rating, seasonal resource data, maintenance history and safety test evidence before making outage resilience predictions.

Every accession must record exact publisher URI, bytes, hash, license terms, page/version, date, uncertainty, primary author and a re-fetch or independent verification receipt. If access fails, the source stays at link/summary status. Do not confuse a source card with an archived original.
