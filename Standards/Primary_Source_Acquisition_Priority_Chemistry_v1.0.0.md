# Chemistry Atlas — original-source preservation priorities after content v1

**Scientific continuity requires authenticated source bytes, not just a URL.** The 487 current bibliographic cards represent navigation and selected scientific descriptions. None has an authenticated original publisher file attached under the strict offline audit.

## First acquisitions and exact evidence needed

1. **NIST Chemistry WebBook SRD 69 source data:** pin public edition and each specific thermochemical table's source/copyright. The Shomate guide defines Cp, H and S calculations; no NIST original Shomate coefficient data is included in this release. Where legally distributable, acquire original file bytes, SHA-256, source citation, phase, state, temperature interval and fit uncertainty.
2. **IUPAC Gold Book, versioned terms:** obtain the original v5.0 single-term JSON for chemical potential, activity, formal/equilibrium electrode potential, and IUPAC electrochemical terminology. Record publication term DOI and rights. Individual IUPAC term materials have CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution conditions, but collection-wide redistribution may have separate restrictions. In this session the direct JSON download failed; **no official bytes were retained**.
3. **Semiconductor properties:** acquire rights-cleared published band gaps, Varshni coefficients, densities of states, mobility and thermal data for source-identified materials and crystal structures, with measurement conditions and uncertainty; do not substitute plausible synthetic silicon parameters and label them NIST measurements.
4. **Electrochemical source data:** obtain source-defined standard states, validated reaction coefficients, ionic activities, cell equilibrium potentials and original reference-electrode protocols under proper rights, but no hazardous manufacturing recipes are included in this package.
5. **Diffraction and spectroscopy:** acquire original publisher spectra and calibrated instrument metadata where licensing permits; retain wavelength, angle conventions, calibration and phase standards separately from computed interpretations.
6. **Independent scientific review:** ask qualified experts to challenge the mathematical models against experimental data, highlight invalid ranges, add counterexamples, and record disagreements rather than automatically editing the source claim into agreement.

## Authentication checklist

For a true authenticated original-source accession preserve: publisher and stable identifier; original response bytes; source transport and retrieval timestamp; license and full legal attribution; file SHA-256; exact edition/date; independent origin verification; transformation code and version; chain of custody; and offline restoration test. A URI or self-reported expected digest alone cannot establish source authenticity or redistribution authority.

## Human-readable minimum kernel

If all online infrastructure disappears, readers must still reconstruct atoms/charge, moles, reaction thermodynamics, equilibrium/activity, electron/semiconductor behavior, diffusion, energy/material interfaces, measurement uncertainties, and the scientific method using the local manual and executable mathematics. They must also know which material coefficients and experiments are unknown. The goal is technological capability and AI research continuity, not undocumented chemical synthesis.
