## Priority 1: Chemical definitions with explicit rights

The **IUPAC Gold Book online fifth edition (2025)** contains precise terminology for activity, standard states, ionic strength, and electrochemical quantities. Individual terms are presented with a **CC BY-SA 4.0** attribution/share-alike license; the full compiled collection may have separate licensing requirements. Acquire a clearly identified individual term's *original publisher bytes* from a trustworthy publisher endpoint, retain the exact date, edition, license and SHA-256, and attribute it properly. **This Chemistry release has not acquired such a file.** The source cards and original explanations are not automatically the licensed original.

## Priority 2: Verified thermochemistry and chemical kinetics

**NIST Chemistry WebBook SRD 69**, data updated 2025, provides measured/compiled thermochemical and spectroscopic data with source and reference-state details. **NIST Chemical Kinetics SRD 17** has its own published data version. Acquire individual legitimate data extracts only under their applicable access and redistribution terms. Preserve original phase, temperature, pressure, uncertainty and literature lineage. A WebBook number without phase and source conditions is not a robust scientific constant.

For each future property entry, preserve: publisher identifier, original units, temperature, phase, standard state, uncertainty, source reference, raw file hash, transformation code and whether it was experimentally measured or computationally inferred. Do not use the newly authored toy Gibbs/van 't Hoff models as surrogate measured values.

## Priority 3: Materials and AI training data

The **Materials Project API** and **PubChem PUG REST** support chemical/material records with different kinds of identifiers and computational/experimental provenance. API source code licensing does not automatically grant rights to every linked materials dataset. Record the exact API/data release, original object, chemical structure or formula semantics, data selection filters, transformation hashes and dataset-specific rights. Before benchmarking scientific AI, hold out publication groups, material families/scaffolds and near-duplicates where relevant, and evaluate against independent science instead of reusing synthetic teaching examples.

## Priority 4: Measurement and scientific acceptance

Source-byte integrity checks prove only equality to an expected digest. Both values can be forged; publisher identity and legal reuse rights must be authenticated separately. A real chemistry reconstruction exercise requires traceable reference material and calibration, appropriate hazard assessment, independently reviewed scientific data, and negative results as well as positive outcomes.

The archive's exact rational charge balance, carrier conservation, Fickian diffusion and absorbance calculations are mathematically reproducible but not a laboratory experiment. They do not instruct hazardous synthesis or semiconductor fabrication.
