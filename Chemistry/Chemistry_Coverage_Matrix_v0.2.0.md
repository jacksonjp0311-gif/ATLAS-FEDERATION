# Chemistry Atlas v0.2.0 — scientific coverage and open acceptance gates

**Version:** active technological-continuity research, not scoped scientific v1 or a working chip-fabrication process.

| Science component | Original reference and executable coverage | What remains missing |
|---|---|---|
| Atomic identity and mole | Atomic counts, SI mole, isotope context | Authentic material samples and measured isotopic abundance |
| Neutral + ionic chemical conservation | Exact rational nullspace and formal charge balance | Real reaction feasibility, phase speciation and chemical safety |
| Gibbs energy, enthalpy, equilibrium | Reaction formation-energy sums, van 't Hoff ln K | Authenticated thermochemical source tables, measurement uncertainty, multi-phase equilibrium |
| Solution activities | IUPAC activity definitions, ionic strength and dilute Debye–Hückel | Concentrated-ion interactions, real solution chemical speciation |
| Kinetics | Arrhenius and first-order examples | Competing mechanism models, authentic independent kinetics experiments |
| Electrochemical energy | Nernst/Faraday foundations and formal charge | Actual battery physics, overpotential, thermal safety, electrolyte kinetics |
| Silicon/semiconductor carriers | Nondegenerate neutrality and mass-action n·p | Band-structure datasets, real dopant ionization, MOS device engineering |
| Diffusion and materials transport | Fick gradient, stable 1D no-flux scheme | Measured D(T), moving interfaces, finite-element multiphysics |
| Spectroscopy/metrology | Beer–Lambert illustrative absorbance, raw-source hash | Calibration spectra, mixture interference, real sample identity |
| Materials databases & AI | PubChem and Materials Project source/data distinctions | Rights-cleared original rows, scientific AI benchmarks, leakage review |
| Industrial chemical processes | Conservation/mass accountability, hazards | Actual unit-operation models, validated industrial process data and safety certification |
| Durable evidence | 18 bibliographic cards in Chemistry; self-contained original chapters | Locally authenticated publisher original bytes = 0, independent expert review = 0 |

**Scope warning:** Passing a numerical model never establishes reaction feasibility, electronics manufacturing ability, legal access to restricted experimental data or an ability to rebuild AI hardware using chemistry alone. The remaining Mathematics/Physics, Electronics and Manufacturing Atlases are essential dependencies.
